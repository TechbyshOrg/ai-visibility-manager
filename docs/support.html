AI Visibility Control Documentation

How to block AI training crawlers, stay visible in AI search, publish llms.txt, and see the visits and sales AI assistants send to your WordPress site.

1. Introduction

AI Visibility Control is a free WordPress plugin that lets you decide which AI crawlers can read your site, publishes an llms.txt file for AI tools, and shows the visits and WooCommerce sales that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and other AI assistants send you. It runs entirely on your site: no account, no API key and no cookies.

Why it matters: AI companies use different crawlers for different jobs. GPTBot collects content for training, while OAI-SearchBot indexes pages for ChatGPT search. With this plugin you can block training and still be found in AI answers.

The plugin has three parts:

  • AI crawler control: choose which of 26 AI crawlers may read your site. The plugin adds the matching rules to your robots.txt file, and can also turn blocked crawlers away or slow down busy ones.
  • llms.txt: an automatic, up-to-date list of your content for AI tools, with an optional full-text llms-full.txt and optional Markdown versions of your pages.
  • AI traffic: reports of the visitors and WooCommerce orders that AI assistants send you, and of the AI crawlers that read your site.

Requirements: WordPress 6.2 or later and PHP 7.4 or later. WooCommerce 8.5 or later for AI sales reports.

2. Installation & Quick Start

  1. In WordPress, go to Plugins > Add New Plugin, search for "AI Visibility Control", then click Install Now and Activate. Or upload the plugin zip under Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin.
  2. Click AI Visibility in the admin menu, or click Settings under the plugin name on the Plugins screen.
  3. Follow the Get started checklist on the Overview tab.
  4. On the AI Crawlers tab, choose a quick setup (for example "Block AI training, allow AI search") and click Save Crawler Settings.
  5. On the llms.txt tab, check the preview and choose which content is listed. Your file is at yoursite.com/llms.txt.
Recommended: use pretty permalinks (for example "Post name" under Settings > Permalinks) so /llms.txt loads correctly on every server.

3. Overview

The Overview tab shows where you stand and what to do next.

Get started checklist

Three steps: choose crawler access, check your llms.txt file, and see AI traffic. Each step has a button that takes you to the right tab. You can hide the checklist once you are done.

Status tiles

AI visits in the last 30 days (compared with the 30 days before), the last AI visit, whether llms.txt is live, and how many AI crawlers are blocked.

Health check

Checks that pretty permalinks are on, that /llms.txt and /robots.txt can be reached, that search engines are not discouraged, and that the site is not in maintenance mode. Problems are listed first, each with how to fix it. The same checks appear in Tools > Site Health.

4. Block AI Crawlers

The AI Crawlers tab lists 26 AI crawlers in three groups. Each one shows an Allowed or Blocked label. Blocked crawlers get a Disallow: / rule, added at the end of the robots.txt file that WordPress creates, so rules from other plugins stay in place.

Quick setup

  • Allow all AI crawlers
  • Block AI training, allow AI search: the most common choice. Your content is not used for training, but AI search tools can still find and cite you.
  • Block all AI crawlers
  • Custom: switch crawlers on and off one by one.
Group Crawlers What they do
AI training GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Amazonbot, Bytespider, meta-externalagent, FacebookBot, AI2Bot, omgili Collect content that may be used to train AI models.
AI search OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, DuckAssistBot, YouBot Index pages so AI assistants can cite and link to them.
User-requested fetchers ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User, MistralAI-User, meta-externalfetcher Open a page when a person asks an AI assistant to. Some providers state these may not follow robots.txt.

AI crawler activity

See which AI crawlers visited in the last 30 days, how many requests each made, how many were refused, when each was last seen, and the most crawled pages. Crawlers are recognised by their User-Agent. Visits served from a page cache or CDN are not counted.

Enforce your choices (optional)

robots.txt only asks crawlers to stay away. Under Enforce Your Choices on the AI Crawlers tab you can go further:

  • Refuse requests from blocked crawlers: blocked crawlers get a short 403 Forbidden response instead of your pages. robots.txt, llms.txt and llms-full.txt are always served, so every crawler can read your rules.
  • Crawl rate limit: each AI training and search crawler may make 10 to 300 requests per minute (your choice). Extra requests get a 429 Too Many Requests response with a Retry-After header. User-requested fetchers such as ChatGPT-User are never limited.

Crawlers are recognised by their User-Agent, which a bot can fake, and pages served from a page cache or CDN never reach WordPress. For complete blocking, also add a User-Agent rule in your cache plugin, CDN or web server. The plugin warns you when a page cache is active.

Advanced: custom rules, Content Signals and preview

Custom robots.txt rules block any other crawler, or a crawler from part of your site only. Write a User-agent: line followed by one or more Disallow: /path lines. Invalid lines are rejected and quoted back to you.

Content Signals add a voluntary Content-Signal line (search, ai-input, ai-train) that states how your content may be used. It does not block anything on its own.

The preview shows the exact rules the plugin adds to robots.txt.

5. llms.txt & llms-full.txt

llms.txt is a plain-text file at yoursite.com/llms.txt that gives AI tools a short, clean list of your content. The plugin creates it automatically and keeps it up to date.

# Site Name

## About

Your About text, or your site tagline.

## Recent Content

- [Post title](https://yoursite.com/post/)
- [Page title](https://yoursite.com/page/)

Settings on the llms.txt tab

  • Content types to list (posts, pages, products and other public types).
  • Your own About text, short descriptions after each link, grouping by content type, and a limit per type.
  • Leave out content marked noindex in Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO, SEOPress or The SEO Framework.
  • Exclude a single post or page with the "Exclude from llms.txt" box in the editor.

llms-full.txt (optional)

Turn on /llms-full.txt to publish the full text of your newest content (100 items by default, up to 500) as Markdown, so AI tools can read it in one request. The file is limited to about 2 MB.

Check first if you use a membership or paywall plugin: WordPress reports members-only content as public, so it would be included.

Markdown versions of pages (optional)

Turn on Serve a Markdown version of each listed page and every page listed in llms.txt is also available as clean Markdown at its address plus .md, for example yoursite.com/about.md, as the llms.txt proposal recommends. A static front page is available at /index.md. Each page also links to its Markdown version in its HTML head, and you can let llms.txt link to the .md versions instead of the normal pages.

The same rules as llms.txt apply: other content types, excluded, noindex and password-protected content has no Markdown version. With plain permalinks the address is ?p=123&tavc_markdown=1.

Always up to date: a saved copy of the file is served so it loads instantly. It is refreshed automatically when you publish, edit or delete content, or when you click Rebuild llms.txt.

6. AI Traffic & WooCommerce Sales

The AI Traffic tab shows visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, Mistral, You.com, Phind, Poe and Duck.ai.

  • Reports for the last 7, 30 or 90 days or all time: a daily chart, a comparison with the previous period, the top AI platforms and the most visited pages.
  • Hidden AI traffic: many AI assistants hide the referrer but tag their links, for example utm_source=chatgpt.com. The plugin recognises these tags too, so ChatGPT visits are not lost as "Direct" traffic.
  • No double counting: the same visitor opening the same page from the same AI platform within 5 minutes is counted once. AI crawlers are never counted as visitors.
  • Latest 20 visits, with how each visit was detected (referrer, UTM tag or browser beacon).

WooCommerce sales from AI

With WooCommerce 8.5 or later and WooCommerce Order Attribution turned on, orders whose visit came from an AI assistant are tagged with the AI source. The AI Traffic tab shows paid orders, revenue (after refunds) and average order value by AI platform, the products AI visitors buy most and the pages where they arrived, and the source appears on the order screen. Compatible with High-Performance Order Storage and the block checkout.

Sites with a page cache or CDN

Cached pages never reach WordPress, so server-side logging cannot see those visits. Turn on Cache-compatible referral tracking under Settings & Tools. It adds a script under 2 KB, with no cookies, that reports only visits coming from AI platforms.

7. Settings & Tools

  • Tracking & data settings: UTM detection, cache-compatible tracking, AI crawler logging, how long logs are kept (forever, 30, 90, 180 days or 1 year), and an optional summary email, sent weekly (Monday morning) or monthly (on the 1st).
  • Maintenance: Rebuild llms.txt, export all AI visits or all AI crawler visits as a CSV file, and send a test summary email.
  • Danger Zone: delete AI traffic logs, delete crawler logs, or reset crawler blocks. Each action asks for confirmation and says what will be deleted.
  • When the plugin is deleted: choose whether all data is removed.

8. Troubleshooting

llms.txt shows a "Page not found" error

Reason: your permalinks are set to "Plain", or WordPress has not refreshed its URL rules yet.

Solution: go to Settings > Permalinks, choose Post name and save. Then click Rebuild llms.txt on the llms.txt tab or the Settings & Tools tab.

robots.txt does not show the AI crawler rules

Reason 1: a physical robots.txt file in your WordPress folder is served instead of the one WordPress creates.
Solution: delete or rename that file (using FTP or your host's file manager), or copy the plugin's rules into it.

Reason 2: your site is installed in a sub-folder, for example example.com/blog/. Crawlers only read robots.txt at the domain root (example.com/robots.txt), and WordPress cannot publish a file there.
Solution: copy the rules shown under AI Crawlers > Advanced > Rules Added to robots.txt into the robots.txt file at your domain root, or ask your host to. Turning blocked crawlers away works without robots.txt.

The health check on the Overview and Status tabs tells you which case applies.

No AI visits are logged

Reason: your pages are served from a page cache or CDN, or no AI assistant has sent visitors yet.

Solution: turn on Cache-compatible referral tracking under Settings & Tools.

9. Privacy

  • The plugin sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses. To avoid counting the same visit twice, a one-way hash is kept for a few minutes and then discarded.
  • AI visit logs contain only the date, the AI platform, the page visited and how the visit was detected. Crawler logs contain the crawler, the page and a daily count.
  • All logs stay in your WordPress database. You choose how long they are kept and can export or delete them at any time.
  • Suggested text for your privacy policy is added under Settings > Privacy.
  • The only outgoing request is the optional deactivation feedback, sent only if an administrator clicks "Submit & Deactivate".

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I block AI training but still appear in ChatGPT and other AI search answers?

Yes. On the AI Crawlers tab, choose "Block AI training, allow AI search" and click Save Crawler Settings. This blocks training crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and CCBot, and keeps search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot allowed, so AI assistants can still find and cite your pages.

Will blocking AI crawlers hurt my Google rankings?

No. Google Search uses Googlebot, which AI Visibility Control never blocks. Google-Extended is a separate setting that only tells Google not to use your content for Gemini training and grounding; Google states it does not affect Search.

Do AI crawlers have to obey robots.txt?

No. robots.txt is a request, not a lock. Major AI companies say their crawlers follow it, but some state that fetchers acting for a user (such as ChatGPT-User or Perplexity-User) may not. Protect private content with a login or server rules, not robots.txt alone.

What is llms.txt, and does it guarantee AI visibility?

llms.txt is a plain-text file at yoursite.com/llms.txt that gives AI tools a short, clean list of your content. AI Visibility Control creates and updates it for you. It makes your content easier for AI tools to read, but it does not guarantee that ChatGPT, Perplexity or any other assistant will show or cite your site.

Can I track ChatGPT traffic in WordPress?

Yes. AI Visibility Control logs visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, Mistral and other AI assistants. It reads the referrer and also recognises tags such as utm_source=chatgpt.com, which catches ChatGPT visits that other analytics tools often report as "Direct".

Can I see WooCommerce sales from ChatGPT and other AI assistants?

Yes, with WooCommerce 8.5 or later and WooCommerce Order Attribution turned on. Orders whose visit came from an AI assistant are tagged with the source, and the AI Traffic tab shows paid orders and revenue by AI platform.

Does it store personal data, use cookies or need an account?

No account, no API key and no cookies. AI Visibility Control does not store IP addresses: a one-way hash is kept for a few minutes to avoid counting the same visit twice. Logs stay in your own WordPress database.

11. For Developers

Filters let you extend the plugin without editing it.

Add a crawler to the AI Crawlers tab

add_filter( 'tavc_bot_definitions', function ( $bots ) {
    $bots['examplebot'] = array(
        'name'     => 'ExampleBot',      // robots.txt User-agent token
        'vendor'   => 'Example Inc.',
        'category' => 'training',        // training, search or user
        'desc'     => 'Collects content for model training.',
        'ua'       => 'ExampleBot',      // User-Agent fragment for activity logging
    );
    return $bots;
} );

Add lines to llms.txt

add_filter( 'tavc_llms_txt_output', function ( $output ) {
    $output .= "\n## Policies\n\n- [Terms](https://yoursite.com/terms/)\n";
    return $output;
} );

Other filters

  • tavc_ai_referrer_domains, tavc_ai_utm_sources: add AI platforms to traffic detection.
  • tavc_llms_txt_post_types, tavc_llms_txt_include_post: choose the content listed in llms.txt and llms-full.txt.
  • tavc_llms_full_txt_output, tavc_llms_full_txt_max_bytes: change llms-full.txt content and its size limit.
  • tavc_custom_robots_rules: change the crawler rules added to robots.txt.
  • tavc_refuse_blocked_crawler, tavc_enforcement_exempt_paths, tavc_rate_limit_per_minute: fine-tune which crawler requests are refused or rate limited. The tavc_crawler_request_refused action fires when a request is refused.
  • tavc_markdown_page_output: change the Markdown version of a page.
  • tavc_log_crawler_visit, tavc_beacon_hourly_limit, tavc_digest_email, tavc_woocommerce_order_ai_source.
